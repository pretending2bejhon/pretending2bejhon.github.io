Icarus Agent

Privacy Policy

Icarus Agent is a personal, single-user automation tool built and operated by Jhonalbert Alvarez solely for his own use. It is not distributed to, and does not collect data from, any other person or account.

What it accesses

Gmail (reads threads, sends replies) and Google Calendar (reads free/busy, creates events) belonging to the operator's own Google account only, through OAuth scopes the operator grants to himself. No other account is ever connected.

How it is used

Email and calendar data is read to draft outbound replies and propose meeting slots for the operator's own outreach work. Every send and every calendar event is approved by the operator before it happens; nothing sends or schedules on its own.

What is never done with it

Data is never sold, never shared with a third party, and never used for advertising. It is not aggregated, resold, or exposed to any other user.

Storage and retention

Operational state and logs are stored locally on the operator's own machine. Nothing is transmitted anywhere except to send an approved message or create an approved event through Gmail's and Calendar's own APIs.
