Most AI looks things up. This remembers.

A private, structured memory for you and your AI. The system on this page is my own, in daily production use. I benchmarked it against the default way of building AI memory, and published the result that went against me.

Book 15 min ↗ How it works ↓

WHAT IT IS · plain files, no vendor

Plain markdown files in folders. No database, no vendor.

Rules live in one boot file. Knowledge is typed (facts, procedures, plans, history) and loaded only when the task needs it.

WHAT IT DOES · every day, on schedule

Briefs me every morning. Consolidates every night.

New facts get filed, stale ones decay. Guardrails sit between the agents and anything destructive. Every claim carries its source.

WHAT YOU GET · the point of it

One place you and your AI answer from.

No re-explaining your business every chat. Answers with sources instead of guesses.

THE BENCHMARK · August 2026, published in full

I tested it against the industry default. RAG came last.

498 questions about long conversations. Same model answering, same scoring, three versions of the same setup. Out of every 100 questions:

78 right

an agent searching my organized files.

75 right

the same files in one folder, no structure at all.

55 right

RAG, the way almost everyone builds AI memory.

Readable files plus search beat RAG by 23 points. That is the part worth buying. The 3 points my structure added over an unstructured folder are too small to call a win, and they cost 27 percent more and answered slower, so the accuracy credit belongs to the search, not to my filing system.

The structure earns its keep somewhere else, and this run did not measure it: you can read the entire memory, every claim names where it came from, and a new file has exactly one right place to live, so two people filing it land in the same folder.
