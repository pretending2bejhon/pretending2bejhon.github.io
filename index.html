THE PROBLEM · knowledge scatters

Every answer lives in somebody's head.

How did we close that deal? Why did that client leave? What did we decide in March? Every business runs on answers like these. Almost none of them are written down where people can find them. They live in inboxes, old chats, and the memory of whoever was there. So the team re-solves problems it already solved. New hires spend months asking. When someone leaves, everything they knew walks out with them.

The answers a business runs on live in heads and inboxes: re-derived every week, lost with every departure.

THE SOLUTION · one private memory

The memory system

Ask your business a question and get the answer in seconds, with the source, no matter whose head it used to live in. I build your company a private, organized memory: the rules, the history, and the decisions behind your work, in one place your people and your AI both answer from. Most AI tools look things up on the fly and forget. This system keeps what your business learns, so every answer makes the next one faster. It runs on your own accounts. What goes in stays yours.

A private, organized memory for your business: people and AI answer from one source, on your own accounts.

HOW IT WORKS

From work happening to the business remembering.

The system sits under the work your team already does. As work happens, the answers, decisions, and fixes get captured, organized, and served back the moment they're needed. To your people and to your AI alike.

Representative operating loop · not a client case

Work happens

PROOF · MEASURED, JULY 2026

Zero losses. Measured, not promised.

This is my own system: every note, client file, and decision of my business lives in it. In July I rebuilt it from the ground up while it kept running. What that means, plainly:

01

What was done

Reorganized my entire business memory into a new structure: 393 files and 2,547 links between them, moved without shutting the system down. Daily work kept running the whole time.

02

How it was checked

A script counted every file and every link before the rebuild, and ran again after. The numbers matched exactly: nothing lost, nothing left pointing nowhere. "Zero losses" is a measurement, not a slogan.

03

What it caught

Eleven automated checks had quietly stopped working. Six guarded security and privacy. All still reported "OK". That silence is how business knowledge actually disappears. Catching it early is what this discipline is for.
